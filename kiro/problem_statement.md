Today, AI systems can generate images that look almost identical to real photographs. While this technology has many positive uses, it also creates serious challenges. Fake images can be used to spread misinformation, mislead people, or misuse someone’s identity, making it harder to trust what we see online.

For most people, it is becoming increasingly difficult to tell whether an image is real or created by AI just by looking at it. At the same time, many existing tools claim to give definite answers, even though no detection system is perfectly accurate. This can lead to false confidence and misuse of such tools.

There is a need for responsible tools that assist people rather than make final decisions for them. Such tools should help users understand whether an image might be AI-generated, while clearly communicating uncertainty and limitations.

This project aims to address that need by providing a system that analyzes images and offers a probability-based authenticity signal, helping users make more informed judgments without claiming absolute certainty.
